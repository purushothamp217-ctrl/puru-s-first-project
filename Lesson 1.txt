Assessment: What an Assistant Can Reach
 Easy
Q1. What is the difference between an answer that fits and an answer that is true?
An answer that fits is relevant to the question and follows the user's request. An answer that is true is factually correct and based on reliable
 information.A good answer should be both relevant and accurate.
Q2. Name the three kinds of AI help taught in this lesson.
Normal Chat
Live Search
Voice
Q3. What is a human check?
A human check means a person reviews the AI's answer and verifies whether the information is correct before using it.
 Intermediate
Q4. A college fest team wants today's ticket price before printing a poster. Should they use normal chat, live search, or voice? Explain why.
They should use Live Search because ticket prices can change at any time. Live Search helps find the latest ticket price and verify the information
 before printing the poster.
Q5. An AI gives a confident film fact for a public poster. What should the team do before using it?
The team should verify the film fact using reliable sources. They should cross-check the information from official websites or trusted sources before
 printing it on the public poster.
 Hard
Q6. For each task, choose chat, live search, or voice. Give one reason for each choice.
1. Write a short caption from details you already know – Normal Chat. It can quickly create a caption using the given details.
2. Find today's train cancellation update – Live Search. It provides the latest information about train cancellations.
3. Hear a reminder while your hands are busy – Voice. Voice allows users to hear reminders without needing to read the screen.
Q7. Write one situation where AI answer needs a human check and one situation where a quick private answer needs less checking. Explain the difference.
An AI answer needs a human check when preparing a public college poster because incorrect information can mislead many people.
A quick private answer, such as asking AI for a simple birthday caption, needs less checking because it is a low-risk and personal task.
The difference is that public information can affect many people, while a simple private answer usually has less impact.
