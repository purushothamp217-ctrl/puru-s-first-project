2. What does it mean to find one weak step in a first attempt?
It means identifying the part of the first attempt that did not work properly or gave an incorrect result. The student should understand the mistake and improve that step
 to get a better result.
3. Why is "it looks fine" not enough before trusting an AI answer?
"It looks fine" is not enough because AI can sometimes provide incorrect or incomplete information. We should check facts, accuracy, 
relevance, and reliability before trusting or using an AI answer.

The student skipped the human review and checking step. They should check whether the timetable matches their learning speed, available time, and daily 
responsibilities before following it.
